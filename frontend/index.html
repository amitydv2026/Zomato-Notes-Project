📂 Bulk Import Notes

Upload a .txt file — each non-empty line becomes one note. Three formats supported:
1. Explicit: Title: My Title | Content: Full content here
2. Colon label: Incident resolved: details here… — text before : becomes title
3. Plain text: first 6 words auto-extracted as title, full line as content
Try sample_import.txt from the project root — it includes all three formats.
